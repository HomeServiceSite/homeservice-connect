Questions

Good to know

Does it cost anything to request a quote?

No. Homeowners can submit a request for free. You decide whether to engage any provider and agree on pricing directly with them.

Are contractors employees of HomeService Connect?

No. Providers are independent businesses. HomeService Connect is a connection and lead-routing platform, not the provider of the home service.

Are providers verified?

Only providers whose review status is explicitly confirmed by HomeService Connect should be described as verified. Always independently check applicable Arizona licensing, insurance and references before hiring.

Do you guarantee a quote or a contractor?

No. Availability depends on participating providers, project details and location. A request does not guarantee a response, quote, price or completed service.
